## 1. Two active appointments put a load on a dock that cannot take its weight

SHP1005 (20,500 kg) is on D3 and SHP1014 (21,000 kg) is on D1; both docks are limited to 20,000 kg. The two unique indexes do not check this.

```sql
select a.appointment_id, a.shipment_id, a.appointment_status, s.load_weight_kg, d.dock_code, d.max_vehicle_weight_kg
from appointments a join shipments s using(shipment_id) join appointment_slots sl using(slot_id)
join docks d on d.dock_id=sl.dock_id
where a.appointment_status in ('PENDING_CONFIRMATION','CONFIRMED','IN_PROGRESS') and s.load_weight_kg > d.max_vehicle_weight_kg
```

## 3. Unload time longer than the booked slot

A 70 or 75 minute unload in a 60 minute slot runs into the next booking on that dock.

```sql
select a.shipment_id, a.appointment_status, s.expected_unload_min,
cast(round((julianday(sl.slot_end_ts)-julianday(sl.slot_start_ts))*1440) as int) as slot_min
from appointments a join shipments s using(shipment_id) join appointment_slots sl using(slot_id)
where a.appointment_status in ('PENDING_CONFIRMATION','CONFIRMED','IN_PROGRESS')
  and s.expected_unload_min > round((julianday(sl.slot_end_ts)-julianday(sl.slot_start_ts))*1440)
```

## 4. Active appointments inside a dock event

A breakdown or capacity reduction overlaps the booked time, and the appointment is unchanged. A capacity reduction states no factor, so its real effect is unknown.

```sql
select a.appointment_id, a.shipment_id, d.dock_code, e.event_type, sl.slot_start_ts, e.event_end_ts
from appointments a join appointment_slots sl using(slot_id) join docks d on d.dock_id=sl.dock_id
join dock_status_events e on e.dock_id=sl.dock_id
 and julianday(sl.slot_start_ts) < julianday(coalesce(e.event_end_ts,'9999-01-01'))
 and julianday(sl.slot_end_ts) > julianday(e.event_start_ts)
where a.appointment_status in ('PENDING_CONFIRMATION','CONFIRMED','IN_PROGRESS')
```
